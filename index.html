Korean Phone & ARC Setup

Before using any Korean app, you must get a Korean phone number and set up identity verification.

Strong Recommendation: Before using any apps, you must register a phone number under your name and get a debit card. Almost all apps require Identity Verification (본인인증) linked to your name and phone number to enable automatic payments.

1 What kind of SIM do you need?

Depending on your visa status and length of stay, your options differ:

  • Prepaid SIM (Passport): Good for short trips, valid up to 90 days. NO identity verification allowed.
  • Postpaid SIM (ARC): Requires Alien Registration Card (ARC) and a Korean bank account. Allows full identity verification for all apps.

2 Identity Verification (본인인증) Apps

  • To verify your identity online, you will use apps like PASS or Toss. Install them first.
  • Select your telecom provider (SKT, KT, LGU+, or MVNO/알뜰폰).

3 Enter Your Details

  • Enter your name EXACTLY as it appears on your ARC. If it is all caps, type all caps.
  • Enter your phone number.
  • Complete the security captcha or SMS verification.

Karrot Market (당근마켓)

The #1 local community and second-hand marketplace in Korea.

Overview: Karrot connects you with locals within a 1-2km radius. You must authenticate your neighborhood to chat and trade.

1 Download and Start

  • Search for '당근마켓' or 'Karrot' on the App Store or Play Store.

2 Phone Verification

  • Karrot doesn't require SNS accounts; it only uses your phone number.
  • Enter the 6-digit code sent via SMS.
  • Note: You can only have one account per phone number.

3 Neighborhood Authentication (동네인증)

  • This is the core feature. You MUST physically be in the neighborhood you select.
  • You must verify your location at least once every 30 days to keep trading.

4 Setting up your Profile

  • Choose a friendly nickname (e.g., 'Friendly Student') and upload a profile picture.
  • A good profile builds trust (Manner Temperature / 매너온도).

5 How to Sell an Item

  • Click the '+' button at the bottom and select 'Sell Item' (내 물건 팔기).
  • Upload clear photos, set a price, and write a detailed description.
  • Be honest about any damages.

Bunjang (번개장터)

Nationwide second-hand trading platform.

1 Safe Payment (번개페이)

  • Bunjang allows nationwide shipping.
  • Always use Bungae Pay (번개페이).
  • The seller will not receive the money until you receive the item and click 'Purchase Confirmed'. This prevents scams.

2 Chat & Inquiries (번개톡)

  • Use the built-in chat (Bungae Talk) to ask the seller questions.
  • If you are not fluent in Korean, it is perfectly fine to write your inquiries clearly in English.

3 Points and Coupons

  • You can earn Bungae Points by completing daily missions or buying items.
  • You can use these points like cash at checkout.
  • Make sure to check the 'MY' tab for event coupons!

A-bly (에이블리) & ZIGZAG

Trendy fashion and beauty shopping platforms.

1 Search & Cart

  • Search for items via keywords.
  • Check the size, color, and reviews before adding them to your cart (장바구니).

2 Registering Coupons

  • Go to 'MY Page' > 'Coupons' > 'Register Coupon' and enter event codes for huge discounts.
  • Coupons have expiration dates, so use them quickly!

3 Using Points

  • At checkout, you can use your accumulated points to cover up to 50% of the total price!
  • Points are earned by writing reviews (photo + text) after confirming your purchase.

Delivered Korea

Your gateway to Korean Fashion worldwide (Shipping internationally).

Option 1 We Buy, We Ship

  • Ideal for zero hassle.
  • Browse ZIGZAG, copy the product URL, and paste it on Delivered Korea. Their personal shoppers buy it for you.
  • When the items arrive at their warehouse, they will send you real photos and consolidate them for international shipping.

Option 2 You Buy, We Ship (Package Forwarding)

  • Full control.
  • Sign up for a FREE Korean warehouse address provided by Delivered Korea.
  • Buy directly on ZIGZAG yourself, and set the shipping address to your free Korean warehouse address.

3 Smart Consolidation & Shipping

  • Waiting to combine 3-4 packages into one box can cut your international shipping cost per item significantly.
  • Choose your international courier when you are ready!

Baemin (배달의민족)

Order food easily using your foreign credit card.

Review Events (리뷰이벤트): Many Baemin restaurants offer free items (drinks, cheese balls) if you promise to leave a 5-star photo review. Look for "리뷰 이벤트"!

1 Switch App to English

  • Baemin won't automatically switch to English.
  • Go to your phone's System Settings > Find the Baemin app > Change the Language to English.

2 The 3-Step Foreign Card Payment Process

  • You no longer need a Korean friend's card! Baemin now uses the NICEPAY gateway to process international cards smoothly.
  • Step 1: On the final order screen, scroll down to Payment Method (결제수단) > Select Other Payment Methods (기타 결제수단) > Tap [ Foreign Card ].
  • Step 2: A NICEPAY pop-up will appear. Select from major global networks: Visa, MasterCard, JCB, or UnionPay.
  • Step 3: Type in your Credit Card Number, Expiry Date, Cardholder Name (exactly as written on the card), and Email. Click the big blue Pay button!
